Three instruments for the working student

Each one is small, fast and single-minded. Try them here: everything below works.

i. Recall

Cards that come back at the right moment

Write a question, answer it from memory, then say how it felt. Recall schedules the next visit so the hard ones return soon and the easy ones rest.

  • Spaced scheduling, no points
  • Keyboard first: space to turn, 1 2 3 to grade
  • Diagrams and images on either side

ii. Plot

A graph you can think with

Type an equation and see it at once. Drag the page to explore, zoom to look closer, and hover to read exact values.

  • Several functions at once, colour-keyed
  • Understands 2x, sin(x), x^2 and more
  • Asymptotes handled gracefully
Specimen · Plot

Drag to pan, scroll to zoom, hover to read values.

iii. Cadence

Focus, with room to breathe

A session timer that draws a single line, and a short list for today. No sounds unless you want them, no streaks, no guilt.

  • Sessions of any length
  • A small list, honestly short
  • Rests suggested, never enforced
