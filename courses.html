Courses

Self-paced courses for learners and classrooms

Every course can be taken alone, or assigned by an educator to a class. Open any entry to read its syllabus. “Foundation” suits students who are new to a subject.

6 courses

Why the rules work, not only how to apply them. Expressions, equations and functions, built up from what students already know. Suits independent study, or assign it unit by unit to a class.

  1. Numbers and their habits
  2. Expressions and equations
  3. Linear relationships
  4. Quadratics and their graphs
  5. Reading functions

Motion, force and energy told through experiments you can picture. Each idea arrives with something to try, which also makes it easy to pair with practical lessons.

  1. Describing motion
  2. Forces and Newton’s laws
  3. Energy and work
  4. Momentum
  5. Waves

A patient approach to a living language: listening first, speaking early, and vocabulary that returns on a schedule through Recall. A good companion to classroom Spanish.

  1. Sounds and greetings
  2. Everyday conversation
  3. Past and future
  4. Reading short stories
  5. Speaking with confidence

Programming as a way of thinking. Small programs, read carefully, then written from a blank page. Designed for first-time programmers, students and teachers alike.

  1. Values and variables
  2. Decisions and loops
  3. Functions
  4. Lists and dictionaries
  5. A small project

How colour behaves, and how to arrange a page so the eye knows where to rest. Looking first, making second. Works as a studio course or a homework companion.

  1. Seeing value
  2. Hue, saturation and mood
  3. Harmony and contrast
  4. Composition
  5. A finished study

Atoms, bonds and reactions, with the reasoning shown at every step so the patterns become familiar. Worked examples first, then practice that mixes topics.

  1. Atoms and the table
  2. Bonding
  3. Reactions and equations
  4. Moles and measurement
  5. Acids and bases
