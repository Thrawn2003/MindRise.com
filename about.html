About

Software that makes a difference

MindRise set out to simplify complex workflows, and to make tools that are accessible, reliable and a joy to use. We have grown from a small team of passionate developers into a name people trust for productivity software.

01

The story so far

  1. Early 2025The beginningTwo engineers with a shared vision started MindRise from a home office, focused on creating utility software that actually works.
  2. 2025First major releaseMindRise launched its first websites and products, taking its place as a competitor in the software utilities field.
  3. Late 2025Enterprise expansionCloud integration services launched, along with reliable servers for development use.
  4. 2026Expanded managementThe team grew to include data entry and design specialists, and a tech team working across multiple technologies.
