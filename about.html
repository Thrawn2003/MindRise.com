About

MindRise, parent company of AAUGS

MindRise is the parent company of AAUGS Corp., the Auroris Advanced Utility and General Software Corporation. We set out to simplify complex workflows, and to make tools that are accessible, reliable and a joy to use.

01

The story so far

  1. Early 2025The beginningTwo engineers with a shared vision started AAUGS from a home office, focused on creating utility software that actually works.
  2. 2025First major releaseAAUGS launched its first websites and products, taking its place as a competitor in the software utilities field.
  3. Late 2025Enterprise expansionCloud integration services launched, along with reliable servers for development use.
  4. 2026Expanded managementThe team grew to include data entry and design specialists, and a tech team working across multiple technologies.
